SchildiChat Revenge is the third generation of SchildiChat, a family of clients for the Matrix protocol.
It features multi-account as a core principle, quick space navigation, a shared codebase for Desktop and Android, and more.
This client is currently still to be considered beta, so depending on your requirements it may or may not be ready for day-to-day use.

The Matrix protocol is a decentralized approach to modern messaging, allowing you to select a server provider of your choice (or even self-host your own server), enabling you to communicate with end-to-end encryption, shared message history across multiple devices, and more.

SchildiChat is open source: https://github.com/SchildiChat/schildi-revenge
More information about the Matrix protocol: https://matrix.org/
